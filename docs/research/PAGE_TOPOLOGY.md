# Elite HCS Homepage Topology

1. Sticky header — logo, primary navigation, phone, enquiry CTA, mobile menu.
2. Hero — one clear H1, supporting copy, two CTAs, participant-focused care image, trust panel.
3. Trust strip — registered provider message, service regions, direct contact.
4. Services — six core NDIS support cards with descriptive copy and links.
5. About / why choose us — brand story, five differentiators, supporting image.
6. Process — enquiry, consultation, tailored support plan.
7. Locations — VIC, NSW, NT and SA coverage with location-specific search copy.
8. Final CTA — callback and phone actions.
9. Footer — contact details, useful links, regions and legal information.

Interaction model: static content with hover/focus states, sticky navigation, mobile menu, and subtle CSS reveal/hover motion. No carousel.
